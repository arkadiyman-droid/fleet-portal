LL97 exposure

Emissions worked out with LL97's own coefficients from what each property reported for its latest year, against its limit (gross floor area × the multifamily limit). Penalty $268 per tonne over. Shown as if every property were on the standard path: rent-regulated and HUD buildings often follow Article 321 (prescriptive measures) or start in 2035 instead, so a figure here is exposure to check, not a bill. The 2030 limit for multifamily differs between sources (0.00335 or 0.00407 t/sq ft); this uses 0.00335, the 2023 rule's figure, and today's use (the cleaner 2030 electricity factor would lower it somewhat).

Usage by property, {{ y.ref }} (utility bills)

kWh, therms and water from the utilities' own bills, which Conservice keeps for every payable. Only the utility's delivery charges are counted: the supply line on the same bill, an ESCO's own invoice (Direct Energy and the like) and the sewer charge repeat the same kWh, therms or gallons. The price per unit is all-in: what was paid for that fuel in {{ y.ref }} (supply and sewer included) divided by the usage. It and "per apt" are shown only where usage is on at least 90% of the utility's bills; a number marked * is a partial year. Readings that price out impossibly low (a utility's data error, e.g. 36M kWh on a $5,497 bill) are left out.
