Changes

settings of the buildings: what changed, when, and who did it

A row is one setting of one building going from one value to another. A change sent through this portal carries the person's name and reason. A change made anywhere else (the vendor's own site, an app, at the controller) is caught by comparing each reading of the settings with the one before, so it shows here too, within about a minute, without a name. Readings are never listed here: only settings.
