The 2010 boxes on Live alert when their status reads Safety (and, on steam, Ignition Failure) on three reads in a row, and when one that was reading stops answering for {{ legacy_dark_min }} minutes. A box alarm the box clears by itself sends nothing; a steam ignition failure with the aquastat or hot water really dropping goes out at once, with that evidence. A steam building that has called for heat 30+ minutes while its aquastat fell 10 F (never rising 5) sends "No heat", with the readings. A hot-water building calling for heat 30+ minutes with its supply 20 F+ under its target the whole last 30 minutes sends "Supply below target". Hot-water buildings never alert on ignition failure. If many boxes stop together you get one message about the connection instead.{% endif %}
