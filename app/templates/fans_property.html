Step 3 · Automatic switching {% if auto_on %}ON{% else %}OFF{% endif %}

When on, the portal does step 2 by itself, once a minute: whenever this property's temperature moves into another band (with the 2° hysteresis), it points the fan groups at that band's slot and logs it below as "automatic". It never changes a schedule's hours, skips any building that is not loaded or not reporting, and does nothing while there is no live outdoor reading. If the portal or Bloom is unreachable, fan groups simply stay on the slot they are on.{% if auto_on and auto_by %} Turned on by {{ auto_by }}, {{ auto_at }}.{% endif %}
